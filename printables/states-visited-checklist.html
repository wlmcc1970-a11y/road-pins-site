States I Have Visited: Free Printable Checklist

All 50 states and DC on one US Letter page, with a box to check and a line for the year of your first visit. Mostly white to save ink.

Download the free PDF Open it to print

How to use it

  • Print it on plain US Letter paper. It is one page.
  • Check a box for each place you have been to.
  • Write the year on the line, so you remember when you went.
  • Count them up at the bottom.

Want it on a map?

Road Pins fills in each place on a real map as you go, and turns it into a poster to frame. Coming soon to Google Play for Android.
